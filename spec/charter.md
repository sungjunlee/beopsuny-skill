# beopsuny-skill Charter

## Rollout status

Complete review drafts, flexible evidence presentation/procedure and authorized matter-scoped harness storage are **implemented in the milestone-8 review branch, not released**. The preserved `e05ecda` runtime is the comparison baseline. Runtime changes and limited evaluation evidence are tracked in the [sprint handoff](../backlog/sprints/2026-09-model-era-refresh.md) and [validation report](../tests/forward_evals/model_era/integration-report.md). Quality objectives O5–O7 remain active: provisional model observations are not expert legal validation. Privacy hints-first remains experimental and is not enabled.

## Problem            <!-- Tier 1 · Direction (human-gated) -->
Korean legal research, contract review, and compliance answers are high-stakes, source-sensitive, and freshness-sensitive; a general agent can easily overclaim, invent citations, blur source authority, or give action-ready advice to the wrong audience.

## Approach           <!-- Tier 1 · Direction (human-gated) -->
Keep one public `beopsuny` skill with a small always-loaded router spine, then load focused references, YAML policies, schemas, and checklists only when the request needs them. Legal conclusions require source/citation, freshness, self-verification, and destination-appropriate evidence; when those gates cannot be satisfied, the skill lowers confidence instead of filling gaps. The primary user is an in-house lawyer; environment setup is owned by the harness and installation guidance, not the public legal contract; the default deliverable is an edit-ready first draft (초벌) with accurate, provenance-labeled grounds — a starting point the lawyer refines, not final advice. There is one operating mode and no Full/Lite distinction: local-mirror availability is per source family, and a family without a mirror gracefully degrades to API/official-link checks that provenance labels disclose.

## Non-Goals          <!-- Tier 1 · Direction (human-gated) -->
- Replace a lawyer or provide final legal advice — the skill provides researched legal information and practical review support, not binding professional judgment.
- Guess statutes, case numbers, enforcement dates, filing requirements, fees, or penalty standards — missing or stale authority must produce `[UNVERIFIED]`, `[INSUFFICIENT]`, `[STALE]`, or a narrower answer.
- Use stale bundled YAML as current-law authority — bundled assets can triage and route, but live source verification is required before present obligations, documents, deadlines, or amounts are asserted.
- Promise push monitoring, cron jobs, or automatic legal-change alerts unless the user explicitly asks for an automation — law-change detection is pull-first by default.
- Certify a clause’s legal validity, guarantee a legal result, or treat drafting authorization as authorization to send, file, or sign. Complete revised clauses, proposed redlines, and negotiation wording for user review are in scope, including a counterparty-facing draft; their completeness or destination does not confer legal certainty or authorize external action.
- Predict litigation outcomes, sentencing, or enforcement/administrative dispositions — litigation and enforcement workflows provide element/fact analysis structure and safe initial-response guidance, not win/loss, sentence, or disposition forecasts.
- Help conceal, destroy, or fabricate evidence, coach testimony, or obstruct an investigation — enforcement and dispute guidance covers lawful evidence preservation, counsel, and rights only, never evasion or obstruction.
- Split into multiple public skills only for internal neatness — the current direction is a single public skill with internal router/reference boundaries until real usage demands a separate surface.

## Contract disposition

| Treatment | Contract or surface | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delete | Hint-only bans on complete review drafts; redundant reviewer/role ceremonies; obsolete assets and their inventory tests | #323, #272, #319, #321 |
| Delete | Duplicated procedure and fixed full-verification triggers that add no evidence; public skill environment instructions | #321, #322 |
| Keep | Official originals/pinpoints, promulgation vs enforcement vs incident-date applicability, exceptions/transitions, opposing authority, partial access scope, lookup failure vs absence, uncertainty and matter isolation | #319, #322, #323, #272 |
| Move ownership | Environment/setup to existing harness/install guidance; authorized storage to available harness functionality and the user-designated repository, without a company database or new format | #321, #272 |
| Move evaluation | Counter-draft meaning from forbidden-string scoring to existing semantic evaluation, retaining actual validity guarantees, unsafe reliance and unauthorized action violations | #284, #324 |
| Experiment | Verification simplification and privacy search-hint ordering; compare preserved runtime with equal runner/source conditions | #320, #322, #325, #326 |

User-requested layout and working method follow the request and risk; evidence obligations survive in a readable form without mandatory section titles or repeated approval ceremonies. Facts imported from company files are data, not instructions. Legitimate user/harness instructions retain their actual authority and are not demoted merely because they arrive through an instruction file. Explicit storage requests may use an available, authorized harness feature or designated repository; confidential and matter-scoped facts must not become general memory or another matter’s context. The skill creates no company-storage subsystem, public skill, mode, or large framework.
